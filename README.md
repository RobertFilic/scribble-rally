# Scribble Rally

Draw a race track with your finger, then race it.

A browser prototype of a top-down racing game for phones:

- **Draw** one loop on the graph paper and it becomes a road. Paint gravel or ice to make it harder, or import a photo of a track drawn on paper.
- **Race** 3 laps in a tilted 3D view (or flat 2D). Hold left or right to steer, hold both to brake.
- **Add stamps**: jumps, boost pads and splash puddles.
- **Collect stars and finish 3 missions** on every track, like "Land 3 jumps" or "Stay on the road for a whole lap".
- **Pick a speed class**: Junior, Rookie, Pro or Elite. Each class keeps its own best time and ghost.
- **Paint your car** in the Garage: colours, patterns, a race number and a pixel doodle. Driver names come from fun word lists, never typed text.
- **Challenge a friend**: a "Beat my time!" link carries your track, your time and your painted ghost car. Friends race your ghost and can challenge you back.
- **Share** the track as a code or a link. Friends race the same track and the same scenery.

Everything is in a single `index.html` (HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with three.js r128 from cdnjs for the 3D view). Best times stay in your own browser.

Status: early prototype for play-testing.
